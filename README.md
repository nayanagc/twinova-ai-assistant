## 📌 Overview

Twinova AI is an intelligent productivity assistant designed to simplify task management, improve focus, and support personal growth. By combining AI-powered planning, behavioral insights, and personalized recommendations, Twinova AI helps users work smarter and achieve their goals more efficiently.

Unlike traditional productivity tools, Twinova AI adapts to user behavior and provides context-aware guidance, making productivity more personalized and effective.

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 AI-powered productivity assistant
- 📅 Smart task management

- 📊 Weekly AI productivity reports

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many students and professionals struggle with task overload, missed deadlines, procrastination, and generic productivity tools that fail to adapt to individual behavior.

Twinova AI addresses these challenges by providing intelligent planning, personalized recommendations, and AI-powered productivity insights.

## 💡 Solution

Twinova AI acts as a digital productivity companion that learns from user interactions, prioritizes tasks intelligently, analyzes productivity patterns, and delivers personalized guidance to help users stay focused and organized.
